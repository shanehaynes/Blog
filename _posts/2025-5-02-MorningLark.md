title: "Morning Lark"
date: 2025-05-02
author: "Shane Haynes"
---
<br>
From muted pink to blue, wings dipping through <br>
Contours and crocuses that the sunrise unveils, <br>
Out of the mountain’s waning mask of shadow, riding <br>
The first tender torrent of <br>
Light above meadows and the murmuring dell,<br>
The lark comes.<br>
<br>
His wing<br>
Plants a nascent day, his motion<br>
Is that of the tiller's plow, we hear<br>
A mellifluous bloom of sprouts of Time.<br>

The head of each sprout is brimming with the potential of our aspirations.<br>

Look! Look! he is climbing the burgeoning blaze<br>
Who knows neither Time nor Error, and under<br>
Whose gaze, undecided, the world, deciding, soars<br>
Into daybreak.<br>

Long now,<br>
The lone lark flutters, the first sparrow<br>
Weaves his sweeping cursive. His grace<br>
Is timeless, too, and sublime. The sun<br>
Shines steadfast, like Plato, over the valley.<br>

Given enough time, we might, we think, hear<br>
The earth erupt into raucous symphony, or memory<br>
Unfurl in brilliance like light through fractured glass.<br>
<br>

The yin to this poem's yang: [Evening Hawk](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/47705/evening-hawk)
